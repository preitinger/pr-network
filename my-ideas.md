## About the angst of stolen values

During almost my whole life, I thought that it was not clever to share ideas too openly that might create a huge profit for myself, one day. Well, this day never gonna happen for too many good ideas because it is very limited what one can achieve acting alone, right?

My new idea *and my hope* is that it is ok when others *steal* an idea because probably we, the good ones, who share openly will achieve much better realizations in much shorter times than the stealer.

And I hope we can find, using the tool "discussion", ways to share the values we create in collaboration in a fair way.
